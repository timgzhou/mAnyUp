\caption{PASTIS test mIoU (GMACs per sample in parentheses) when choosing both \emph{where} and \emph{when}: each method picks $n$ spatial locations and, at each, one or two seasons of monthly crops ($\approx n \times \frac{12}{4} \times M$ or $n \times \frac{12}{2} \times M$ additional tokens, $M$ modalities). Grid: a regular lattice of locations, every 4th / 2nd month. Random: random locations and seasons. Entropy + learned: the highest-entropy locations of the model's own crop-free pass, each with the seasons of lowest learned score (trained on the true per-season loss ranking; no labels at test). Entropy + attention: the same locations, each with the seasons the readout attends to most in the crop-free pass (no trained scorer). Entropy, all months: the same locations with every month (space only, for reference). Global threshold: the same, but a location is picked when its entropy exceeds the validation split's $(1 - n / N)$ quantile ($N$ candidate windows), so each tile gets as many crops as it needs; the GMACs are the test-set average. Every row uses full self-attention over season and crop tokens except cross attention: crop tokens attend to the season tokens and to the tile's other crops, season tokens never to crops, so the crop-free pass is reused and only the crop tokens are computed again. Entropy GMACs include the crop-free pass. Fixed-resolution references: S16 seasonal .429 (1.7), S16 monthly .462 (6.3), S8 seasonal .525 (10.6), S8 monthly .572 (53.7), S4 seasonal .570 (100.2), S4 monthly .614 (673.2).}
